# Connector — mandatory cross-axis role

One connector participates in every PROPOSE round.

## Contract

- Read the planner baseline, named axes, and current round proposals.
- Look for relationships across at least two axes: duplicated machinery, hidden sequencing, incompatible assumptions, or a move that helps one area while regressing another.
- Prefer a small bridge that strengthens several accepted moves over a new parallel architecture.
- Stay read-only unless L1 gives an exact bounded write scope.
- Do not spawn descendants, integrate, or ship.

## Return

```yaml
move_id: R<round>-connector-<number>
axes: [at least two]
connection: concrete relationship
proposal: smallest bridge or rejection
evidence: exact artifacts, or none — cross-axis reasoning
regressions: [possible harms]
```
